# Evaluation Findings — SCOBY Watch

**Project:** SCOBY Watch  
**Final evaluation:** GroupShuffleSplit by `batch_id`  
**Final model:** Gradient Boosting  
**Status:** Final evaluation completed  

---

## 1. Purpose

The evaluation stage measures the model's ability to approximate
rule-generated targets on batches that do not appear in the training set.

The evaluation does not measure biological readiness or food safety.

## 3. Final Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Accuracy | **0.995918** |
| Weighted F1-score | **0.995921** |

Approximately 99.59% of test observations are predicted in agreement with
the rule-generated target.

Correct interpretation:

```text
The model closely approximates deterministic labels on unseen batches.
```

Incorrect interpretation:

```text
The model accurately understands real kombucha fermentation.
```

---

## 4. Relationship to the Random Split

The random-row split produces a similarly high score.

However, that split carries a batch-leakage risk because observations from a
single batch trajectory can appear in both subsets.

The group split is more appropriate for final evaluation because:

- complete batches are separated;
- trajectories are not distributed across both train and test;
- the result is more defensible for repeated observations per batch.

---

## 5. Confusion Matrix

The final-model confusion matrix is stored as a plot artifact.

The general pattern shows that most observations fall on the main diagonal.

The remaining errors should be interpreted as failures to approximate nearby
rule boundaries, not as failures to understand biological processes.

---

## 6. Interpreting Near-Perfect Performance

The near-perfect score is expected because:

1. the target is generated by deterministic thresholds;
2. the model features include the main threshold variables;
3. tree-based models are suitable for piecewise boundaries;
4. the synthetic trajectories use controlled noise;
5. the classes do not depend on hidden biological factors.

Near-perfect performance is evidence that the pipeline behaves as designed,
not evidence of biological novelty.

---

## 7. Model Comparison

Tree-based models substantially outperform KNN and Logistic Regression.

Interpretation:

- Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting approximate the rule boundaries;
- Logistic Regression is constrained by linear separation;
- KNN is affected by the distance structure;
- scaling assists KNN and Logistic Regression but does not change the nature
  of the target boundaries.

---

## 8. Confidence

Gradient Boosting provides `predict_proba`.

Confidence is used by the Hybrid Decision Layer.

Reference-payload example:

```text
rule_status = balanced
ml_status = balanced
ml_confidence ≈ 0.9998
final_status = balanced
```

High confidence must not be interpreted as calibrated biological certainty.

---

## 9. Threshold 0.70

Hybrid threshold:

```text
ml_confidence < 0.70
→ manual_check
```

The threshold is selected as a conservative heuristic for the software
prototype.

The following have not yet been performed:

- sensitivity analysis;
- threshold optimization;
- probability calibration;
- reliability diagrams;
- expected calibration error analysis.

---

## 10. Threats to Validity

### Construct validity

The target is a rule-generated status.

### Internal validity

The generator depends on formulas, noise, clipping, and rule order.

### External validity

Seven recipes do not represent all kombucha variations.

### Model validity

The metrics measure label approximation.

### Sensor validity

pH and Brix remain simulated.

### System validity

The evaluation runs on a local prototype.

### Safety validity

No microbiological or food-safety testing has been performed.

## 12. Conclusion

Gradient Boosting successfully approximates rule-generated labels with a very
high score on batches that do not appear in the training set.

The result supports the readiness of the software pipeline for model serving
and hybrid integration.

The result does not support claims of biological prediction or food-safety
validity.
