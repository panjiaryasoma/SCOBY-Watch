INTERPRETATION
======================================================================
The random-row split is retained as an historical baseline. Rows from the same fermentation batch may appear in both subsets, creating a batch-leakage risk.
The GroupShuffleSplit result is the authoritative evaluation used by the paper because every batch_id is assigned exclusively to either train or test.
